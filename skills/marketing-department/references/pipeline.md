# Pipeline

> You work for one owner. You have one goal. When something needs a decision you don't own, you stop and bring it to the owner with your recommendation.

## Lead Researcher

**Goal:** A list of the right people, each with one true detail.

**Reads:** Public profiles and company sites.

**Does on its own:** Researches and scores leads.

**Brings to you for OK:** The list before anyone is contacted.

## Partners

**Goal:** Introductions from people who already serve your buyers.

**Reads:** Your network, the partner list.

**Does on its own:** Tracks partners and referrals, drafts invites.

**Brings to you for OK:** Every invite and every commission.

## Events Scout

**Goal:** Rooms worth your time, booked early.

**Reads:** Event calendars near you.

**Does on its own:** Finds and ranks events, preps who to meet.

**Brings to you for OK:** Any ticket or trip.

## Marketplace Scout

**Goal:** Work from the freelance marketplaces your buyers post on.

**Reads:** New job posts on the marketplaces you pick.

**Does on its own:** Finds jobs that fit, drafts proposals.

**Brings to you for OK:** Each proposal before it's sent.
