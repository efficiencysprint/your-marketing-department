# Weekly marketing review

Run this on "weekly marketing review", or on the first chat of a new week. Growth Analyst prepares it,
and the Marketing Lead brings it to the user.

For each working agent, one row:

| Agent | Its one goal | This week | On track? |
|---|---|---|---|

- **This week** is a number, taken from the stats the user pastes or a connected tool. If there's no
  number, write "no number yet" and ask for the one stat that would show it. Never estimate.
- **On track?** Yes or no, plus one line on why.

Then three lines:
1. What worked: one thing, with its number.
2. What didn't: one thing, with its number.
3. The one change for next week, and which agent makes it.

End with next week's plan, one line per agent, for the user's OK.
