# Set up my marketing

Run this once, in the first chat, before any other work. Ask one question at a time, and keep each
answer short. Seven questions:

1. **Your company.** What you sell, and to whom (one line each).
2. **Your best client.** Who they are, and why they're your best.
3. **This month's one goal**, as a number: calls booked, sign-ups, replies, revenue.
4. **Your channels today:** LinkedIn, X, Threads, Reddit, email, video, webinars, events, freelance
   marketplaces. Only these agents start; the others wait.
5. **Your voice.** Paste three things you wrote and liked: a post, an email, a page. Brand Voice keeps
   your real phrases from them.
6. **Your tools.** The apps you work in: email, calendar, CRM, social, analytics, files. In Claude, turn on a connector for each one you can (Settings → Connectors). The agents read from what's connected, and from anything you paste. Nothing is sent without your OK.
7. **What always comes to you.** The default: everything that goes out. Name anything else that must come
   to you first (prices, new people, anything over an amount).

Then write the **Marketing brief**, one page:
- Company, offer, best client
- This month's goal (the number)
- Channels on, and the agents that work on them
- Tools connected (and the ones to paste from)
- Voice notes: 5–10 of the owner's own phrases, and the words to avoid
- What always comes to the owner

Where to keep it:
- Working in a folder: save it as `marketing-brief.md` there.
- In claude.ai: suggest adding it to a Project's files, so every chat in that Project starts with it.

Last, the Marketing Lead proposes this week's plan from the brief and asks for the user's OK.
