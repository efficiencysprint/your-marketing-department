# Your Marketing Department, 24/7 in Claude

A Marketing Lead and 25 agents on one org chart, with you on top.

Try these on day one:
- "Write this week's LinkedIn post about the question my clients ask most."
- "Find 20 people who fit my best client, each with one true detail."
- "Turn my last newsletter into posts for LinkedIn, X and Threads."

I run my company with more than 100 AI agents across a team of three. This is the marketing part of it.

Each agent has one goal, and knows what it can do on its own and what it brings to you first. That's what turns a pile of prompts into a department.

## Add it to Claude

- **Claude desktop app:** download [your-marketing-department.plugin](https://github.com/luba-hub/your-marketing-department/releases/latest/download/your-marketing-department.plugin), open it with Claude or drag it into a chat, then click Install.
- **claude.ai in a browser:** Settings → Capabilities → Skills → Upload, then pick [your-marketing-department.zip](https://github.com/luba-hub/your-marketing-department/releases/latest/download/your-marketing-department.zip).

Then start a new chat and tell the Marketing Lead your goal for this month.

## The department

You approve what goes out → **Marketing Lead** (one goal per agent, tracked weekly)

| Team | Agents |
|---|---|
| Voice & Copy | Content Writer, Brand Voice, Offer Architect |
| Social | LinkedIn, X, Threads, Reddit |
| Search & PR | SEO Strategist, PR Desk, Website Lead |
| Email | Email Design, Email Automation, Cold Email |
| Video | Video Editor, Reel Covers, Webinar Producer |
| Pipeline | Lead Researcher, Partners, Events Scout, Marketplace Scout |
| Send & Inbox | Send Desk, LinkedIn Inbox, Onboarding Desk |
| Numbers | Growth Analyst, Quality Gate |

Each agent has a Goal, what it Reads, what it Does on its own, and what it Brings to you for OK.
